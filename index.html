I am a master's student at Mila - Quebec AI Institute, advised by Elvis Dohmatob. Prior to this, I studied Mathematics at McGill University.

Broadly speaking, my research interests are in theoretical machine learning. The main goal of my research is to demystify weird / surprising empirical phenomena that occur in practise, and provide theoretical explanations for them.

Nowadays, I'm mainly thinking about principled ways of designing data-efficient models that generalize well, specifically in a regime where compute resources are abundant but training data is limited.
